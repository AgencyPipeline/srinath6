Questions

Where does the data come from?

From the project pages and quarterly filings developers must publish on the MahaRERA website: units, units sold, money received, construction progress and plan changes. We show the source for every figure.

What if a project hasn't filed its report?

The tracker shows it as not filed for that quarter. We never fill gaps with estimates.

Do you show selling prices?

Not in the first version. MahaRERA filings don't include price per square foot, so the tracker focuses on sales speed and stock. Price data can be added later.

Which states do you cover?

Maharashtra first. Other states can be added as a paid extension where their RERA portals publish similar data.

Do you store buyers' personal details?

No. We only collect project-level information and never store or show buyer names or contact details.

Who owns the system?

You do. The code and data are yours, hosted in India, in your own cloud account if you prefer.
